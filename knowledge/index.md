# Knowledge

**A human-readable collection of what is implemented in the code.** One document per concept, in the
operator's words rather than the schema's: what the thing is, what was ruled about it and when, which
measurement settled each ruling, and what was tried and rejected on the way.

These pages are the PROSE HOME. `ontology/concepts/<name>.yaml` is the declaration — skeleton,
bindings, parameters — and carries no paragraphs. The two are written in one act and read in
sequence: the page first, whole, to form a view of what a concept does; the declaration second,
compared against that view. Interleaving them in one file made both unreadable as wholes and the
comparison impossible, which is what this plane exists to fix
(`meaning-as-code#decisions/PROPOSED-2026-10-05_prose-formal-separation.md`).

Nothing here is a projection. `ontology/concepts/<name>.md` is the generated face of the declaration
and is a different artifact; this is authored.
