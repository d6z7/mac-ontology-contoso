The meaning you authoredconcept

Margin — a measure
Gross profit: net revenue minus cost of goods, per order line, converted to one base currency, then summed.
How it computes
SUM( Quantity × (NetPrice − UnitCost) × ExchangeRate ) — net revenue − cost of goods, converted to USD
The judgment — contested, so disclosed
  • Gross, not net. Only cost of goods (UnitCost) is subtracted; operating & overhead costs aren’t in the data. margin.gross_basis_disclosed
  • Revenue is net of discount — the same basis as Sales, so the two measures agree. margin.net_revenue_side
  • Convert per line before summing — never add profit across five currencies raw. margin.single_currency_basis
Grounded on real columns
sales · NetPrice · UnitCost · Quantity · ExchangeRate
