# Store.premises_not_place

> GENERATED by tools/mac_rules.py from `ontology/rules/Store.premises_not_place.yaml` — never authored.

A store is the premises Contoso sells from: a shop standing on one place for one trading period, with its own name, floor area, opening date and closing date. It is part of a location, not the location itself — one place may hold a succession of premises over time, each of them a store. So one store is counted and listed by its premises, and a count, list or sum over stores is a different question from the same figure over places; an answer that could be read at either level says which level it used. The site reading (one row per place, whatever premises it held) is the other option.
