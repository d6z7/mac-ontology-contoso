# Currency.whose

> GENERATED by tools/mac_rules.py from `ontology/rules/Currency.whose.yaml` — never authored.

A currency is reached by three routes: the currency a sale was paid in, or one of the two currencies of an exchange rate — its source (the currency it converts from; for a sale, the currency the amounts are stored in) or its target (the currency it converts to; for a sale, the sale's own currency). A question that reaches a currency without saying which — from a sale, a store, a site or an exchange rate — is answered on the currency the sale was paid in; a question may name the rate's source or target currency instead. The answer says which currency it used.
