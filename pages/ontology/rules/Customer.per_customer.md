A figure "per customer" divides by a count of customers, and which customers it counts is a choice. By default it counts the buyers: the distinct customers on the sales lines the figure is summed over, so revenue per customer by country divides each country's revenue by the customers who bought in that country. The other options count the customers active on the reference date, or every customer on the register. The answer says which customers it divided by.
